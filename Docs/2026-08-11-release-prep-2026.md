### Nearby embargo leak (found during screenshot pass; fix in working tree)
- Symptom: embargo-locked fresh install, map's nearby card showed art piece "Aeshtah" — region-sourced. `NearbyViewModel` and `NearbyCardViewModel` fetch art/camps/events with `ArtFilter(region:)`/`CampFilter(region:)`/`observationFilter(region:)` and distance-sort, with no embargo check. Presence + rank in a proximity list narrows embargoed placement — same class as the July `UserMapViewAdapter` leak fixed by `MapRegionAnnotationFilter` (`836c34d`).
- Decision (user-confirmed): gate like the map. While locked, art/camp observations emit nothing; events filter per occurrence via `BRCEmbargo.canShowLocation(for:)` (art-located events ride the art tier). Both VMs subscribe to `.BRCEmbargoDidClear` and restart observations on unlock (flag is UserDefaults-read-imperatively; nothing re-reads it otherwise).
- Tests: delegated to Opus subagent (patterns: `EmbargoTierTests`, mock-date keys, no force unwraps).
