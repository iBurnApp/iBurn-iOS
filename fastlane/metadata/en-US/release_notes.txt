Welcome to iBurn 2026. This one's a big one.

• Apple Watch app! Browse camps, art, and events and see your pins on the map, right from your wrist. Favorites sync with your phone.
• Official 2026 city map, built from Black Rock City's own survey data.
• Favorite a single event occurrence instead of the whole series. Just the Thursday sunrise set, please.
• Make the tab bar yours: reorder tabs, hide the ones you never open.
• Rebuilt search, with scopes, day and time filters, and favoriting from the results.
• "Right Now" shows what's happening around you at this very moment, plotted on the map.
• Long-press the map to drop a pin and explore what's nearby from that spot. Great for planning tomorrow from the shade.
• Walk and bike times instead of raw distances, because nobody thinks in kilometers out there.
• Much faster first launch, and a fresh look for iOS 26.

Camp locations unlock on their own once the org releases them. No passcode hunting required.
